Quick tips

Five habits that keep your accounts safer.

  1. Use a different password for every account.
  2. Make passwords long. A phrase of four or more random words works well.
  3. Use a password manager so you do not have to remember them all.
  4. Turn on two-step verification for your email and university accounts.
  5. Never share a password, even with friends.
